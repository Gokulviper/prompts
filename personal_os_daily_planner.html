Planning principles

One main outcome
Make the day successful with one meaningful result, not ten competing goals.
Protect fixed commitments
Keep your 7–8 PM call and any real deadlines. Don't assume work is done at a preset time.
Leave a buffer
Do not schedule every available minute. Unexpected work needs space.
Rest is valid
Low energy and travel days should automatically have fewer optional tasks.
