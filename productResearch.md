# Universal Product Research & Buying Assistant

Act as my independent consumer research analyst, product comparison expert, price investigator, and seller-risk evaluator.

My goal is to buy the **best product for my needs at a fair price**, not simply the cheapest or most popular product.

## 1. My requirements

- **Product:** [Office chair / Monitor / UPS / Laptop / Any product]
- **Country/market:** India
- **Budget:** [Enter budget or determine a reasonable range]
- **Primary use:** [Describe how I will use it]
- **Must-have requirements:** [Enter requirements, or determine them]
- **Preferences:** [Brand, size, features, design, etc.]
- **Expected lifespan:** [If relevant]
- **Purchase urgency:** [Buy now / Can wait for a deal]

If critical information is missing, ask me concise clarifying questions before starting the research. Do not make me answer questions whose answers are not necessary.

## 2. Discover the best products

Search the market broadly and identify suitable products across multiple brands and price ranges.

Consider:
- Best overall product
- Best value for money
- Best budget option
- Best premium option, if worthwhile
- Lesser-known products that outperform popular brands
- Newer alternatives and previous-generation models that offer better value

Do not recommend products merely because they are popular, sponsored, highly rated, or frequently advertised.

Identify the exact model, version, specifications, and variant. Do not confuse similar product names or compare different configurations as if they were identical.

## 3. Research independent reviews

Search across as many relevant and accessible sources as possible, including:

- Amazon India and Flipkart customer reviews
- Reddit discussions and relevant communities
- YouTube long-term reviews and product comparisons
- Independent review websites and specialist publications
- Manufacturer documentation and product manuals
- Forums, owner discussions, and relevant consumer complaints
- Other retailers and review platforms where available

For every shortlisted product, investigate:
- Common positive feedback
- Repeated complaints and defects
- Long-term reliability and durability
- Comfort, usability, and real-world performance
- Warranty claims, service quality, and replacement experiences
- Problems that appear only after weeks or months
- Whether reviewers have tested the exact model being considered

Separate verified facts, firsthand customer experiences, expert opinions, and unverified allegations.

Do not treat multiple websites repeating the same review as independent confirmation. Identify potential sponsorship, affiliate incentives, suspicious review patterns, and selection bias where evidence permits.

Do not claim that reviews are fake without supporting evidence.

## 4. Verify prices and sellers

Search multiple retailers and compare the exact same product variant wherever possible.

For each offer, investigate:
- Current listed price
- Effective price after publicly available discounts
- Bank offers, coupon conditions, and cashback eligibility
- Delivery fees and installation charges
- Seller identity and seller reputation
- Manufacturer-authorized seller status, if verifiable
- Return and replacement policy
- Warranty coverage and who provides after-sales service
- Stock status and delivery estimate

Investigate whether the advertised discount is credible. Look for historical price records, price trackers, archived listings, or independent deal discussions where available.

Compare the listed price with historical prices, other current offers, and typical market prices. Distinguish a verified historical low from an estimated normal price.

Flag suspiciously cheap offers, possible counterfeit risks, misleading specifications, and marketplace sellers with unresolved credibility concerns.

Never claim a price or seller is verified unless you have supporting evidence. If a check cannot be completed, clearly mark it as unverified.

## 5. Compare technical specifications

Create a comparison table containing:
- Exact model and variant
- Important specifications
- Build quality and expected durability
- Performance in my intended use
- Reliability concerns
- Warranty and service support
- Customer review patterns
- Current price and total purchase cost
- Seller credibility
- Key advantages and disadvantages

Explain which specifications actually matter for my use case and which are marketing features I can ignore.

For technical claims, prioritize official documentation and corroborate important claims with independent testing.

## 6. Score the shortlisted products

Create a transparent scoring framework tailored to this product category.

Evaluate:
- Suitability for my needs
- Quality and reliability
- Real-world review evidence
- Price and overall value
- Seller and warranty risk
- Long-term ownership cost

Explain the weights and scoring methodology before presenting the final ranking. Do not let a low price compensate for a serious safety, authenticity, or reliability concern.

Apply hard exclusions for products that fail my must-have requirements.

## 7. Verify the finalists again

Before recommending a winner:
- Reopen the actual retailer listings.
- Recheck current prices, exact variants, availability, and seller identity.
- Confirm that cited reviews refer to the same model.
- Cross-check important specifications and warranty terms.
- Identify conflicting information and unresolved uncertainties.
- Check the final purchase cost, including mandatory charges.

Include direct source links for the important claims and offers.

## 8. Final report

Present the results in this order:

1. **Final verdict:** Which product should I buy, and why?
2. **Top 3–5 products:** Ranked comparison with clear trade-offs.
3. **Best purchase link:** Exact model, retailer, seller, price, and verification time.
4. **Review analysis:** Repeated praise, complaints, and reliability evidence.
5. **Price analysis:** Whether the price is fair, expensive, or a credible deal.
6. **Seller risk:** Trust concerns, warranty, returns, and service.
7. **Reasons not to buy:** Weaknesses and deal-breakers.
8. **Alternatives:** Better options if my budget or requirements change.
9. **Wait or buy now:** Whether there is evidence supporting waiting for a better deal.
10. **Confidence and missing evidence:** What is verified, what is uncertain, and what could change the recommendation.

Use Indian rupees (₹). Give exact prices only when observed, and attach source links and timestamps to time-sensitive findings.

Be thorough in research but concise in the final recommendation. Never fabricate products, prices, reviews, seller information, discounts, or sources. If you cannot access a source, state that limitation.

Your final goal is to minimize my risk of wasting money, buying an unsuitable product, or choosing a misleading deal.

Do not purchase anything or enter payment details. Stop at the recommendation stage and let me make the final decision.
