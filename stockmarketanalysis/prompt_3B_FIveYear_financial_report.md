# Prompt 3B: Five-Year Financial Statement Analysis

Act as a forensic financial analyst and institutional equity research analyst.

Analyze the following company:

Company: [COMPANY NAME]
Ticker: [TICKER]

Analyze at least the last 5 completed financial years and the latest reported quarterly results.

## 1. Income statement analysis

Analyze:
- Revenue growth.
- Cost of revenue.
- Gross profit and margin.
- Operating expenses.
- Operating income.
- EBITDA.
- Net income.
- EPS growth.
- Stock-based compensation.

Identify the reasons for significant changes.

## 2. Balance sheet analysis

Analyze:
- Cash and equivalents.
- Short-term investments.
- Accounts receivable.
- Inventory.
- Property, plant and equipment.
- Goodwill and intangible assets.
- Current liabilities.
- Long-term debt.
- Shareholders' equity.

Assess balance sheet strength.

## 3. Cash flow analysis

Analyze:
- Operating cash flow.
- Investing cash flow.
- Financing cash flow.
- Capital expenditure.
- Free cash flow.
- Cash conversion ratio.
- Working capital changes.

Explain whether accounting profits translate into cash.

## 4. Financial ratios

Calculate:
- Gross margin.
- Operating margin.
- Net margin.
- ROE.
- ROA.
- ROIC.
- Current ratio.
- Quick ratio.
- Debt-to-equity.
- Net debt/EBITDA.
- Interest coverage.
- FCF margin.

## 5. Quality of earnings

Investigate:
- One-time gains and expenses.
- Accounting adjustments.
- Stock-based compensation.
- Acquisition-related expenses.
- Goodwill impairment.
- Revenue recognition.
- Customer prepayments.
- Working capital distortions.

## 6. Five-year trend analysis

Show historical trends using tables and charts.

Identify:
- Improving metrics.
- Deteriorating metrics.
- Cyclical patterns.
- Structural changes.

## 7. Financial red flags

Identify:
- Increasing debt.
- Deteriorating cash flow.
- Margin compression.
- Share dilution.
- Receivables growth.
- Inventory accumulation.
- Declining ROIC.

## 8. Conclusion

Assess:
- Financial strength.
- Earnings quality.
- Cash-flow quality.
- Balance sheet sustainability.
- Long-term financial stability.

Use official annual reports and quarterly filings.

Cite every material financial figure. Separate reported figures from your calculations and estimates. Never fill missing data with guesses.
