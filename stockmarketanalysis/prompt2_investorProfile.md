# Prompt 2: Investor Profile and Extensive Investment Overview

You have already completed the basic company research.

Now collect the investor's requirements.

First, identify which information I have already provided. Do not ask me to repeat it.

Ask for any missing information one question at a time:

1. Investment horizon.
2. Total investment capital.
3. Risk tolerance.
4. Investment style: Growth, Value or Blend.
5. One-time investment or periodic investment.
6. Investment objective.

Once you have sufficient information, prepare an extensive investment overview.

## Extensive analysis

### A. Business quality
- Business model sustainability.
- Revenue diversification.
- Competitive advantages.
- Customer concentration.
- Industry positioning.

### B. Financial quality
- Historical revenue growth.
- Profitability.
- Cash generation.
- Debt and liquidity.
- Capital allocation.
- Shareholder dilution.

### C. Growth potential
- Industry growth.
- Product expansion.
- New markets.
- Technology opportunities.
- Management guidance.

### D. Management quality
- Leadership track record.
- Capital allocation.
- Guidance accuracy.
- Insider ownership.
- Governance.

### E. Competitive landscape
- Main competitors.
- Relative market positioning.
- Market share.
- Pricing power.
- Competitor threats.

### F. Valuation overview
- Historical valuation multiples.
- Forward P/E.
- EV/EBITDA.
- Price-to-sales.
- Preliminary valuation concerns.

### G. Investment risks
- Financial risks.
- Business risks.
- Industry risks.
- Regulatory risks.
- Macroeconomic risks.

### H. Investor-specific considerations
- Investment horizon.
- Capital allocation.
- Position sizing.
- Currency risks.
- Tax implications where relevant.

## Output

Provide:
1. Executive investment summary.
2. Business quality assessment.
3. Financial health assessment.
4. Growth opportunities.
5. Competitive analysis.
6. Valuation overview.
7. Risk register.
8. Important upcoming events.
9. Investment thesis.
10. Information gaps requiring deeper research.

At the end, ask me which deep research modules I want to execute.

Do not invent valuation conclusions without a proper financial model.
