# Prompt 3D: Competitor Valuation Comparison

Act as an institutional equity research analyst specializing in relative valuation.

Analyze:

Target company: [COMPANY NAME]
Ticker: [TICKER]

Identify 5–8 relevant publicly listed competitors.

## 1. Competitor selection

Choose companies based on:
- Similar business model.
- Similar revenue streams.
- Industry exposure.
- Customer categories.
- Geographic presence.
- Competitive overlap.

Explain why each company belongs in the peer group.

Do not include irrelevant companies simply to increase the peer count.

## 2. Financial comparison

Compare the latest available financials:

- Revenue.
- Revenue growth.
- Gross margin.
- Operating margin.
- EBITDA margin.
- Net income.
- EPS growth.
- Operating cash flow.
- Free cash flow.
- Capital expenditure.
- Net debt.

Normalize reporting periods and currencies where possible.

## 3. Valuation comparison

Calculate:
- Market capitalization.
- Enterprise value.
- Trailing P/E.
- Forward P/E.
- EV/EBITDA.
- EV/Revenue.
- Price-to-sales.
- Price-to-free-cash-flow.
- PEG ratio.

Clearly identify GAAP and adjusted metrics.

## 4. Growth and profitability comparison

Compare:
- Historical revenue CAGR.
- Expected revenue growth.
- EPS CAGR.
- ROIC.
- Free cash flow margin.
- Operating margin trends.

## 5. Competitive positioning

Investigate:
- Market share.
- Technology advantage.
- Pricing power.
- Switching costs.
- Customer retention.
- Distribution advantage.
- Competitive threats.

## 6. Valuation premium or discount

Determine whether the target company deserves:
- Premium valuation.
- Similar valuation.
- Discount valuation.

Support the conclusion with financial evidence.

## 7. Peer-based valuation

Calculate an implied valuation using:
- Median peer P/E.
- Median EV/EBITDA.
- EV/Revenue.
- Relevant growth-adjusted multiples.

Account for differences in growth, profitability, leverage and business mix.

## 8. Final comparison

Create:
- Competitor financial comparison table.
- Valuation comparison table.
- Revenue growth chart.
- Profitability chart.
- Cash-flow comparison.
- Relative valuation analysis.

Identify where the target company is stronger and weaker.

Use current market prices and recent official financial statements.

Cite all data and clearly explain differences in reporting periods.
