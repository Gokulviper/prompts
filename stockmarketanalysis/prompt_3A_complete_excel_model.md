# Prompt 3A: Complete Excel Valuation Model

Act as a senior investment banking financial modelling analyst.

Build a comprehensive, formula-driven Excel valuation model for:

Company: [COMPANY NAME]
Ticker: [TICKER]
Investment horizon: [HORIZON]
Investment capital: [AMOUNT]

## 1. Historical financial statements

Collect at least 5 years of historical financial data.

Include:
- Income statement.
- Balance sheet.
- Cash flow statement.
- Revenue by segment.
- Geographic revenue.
- Share count and dilution.
- Capital expenditure.
- Debt and interest expense.

Use official filings wherever possible.

## 2. Financial modelling

Build projections for the next 5 years.

Include:
- Revenue growth by segment.
- Gross margin.
- Operating margin.
- EBITDA.
- Net income.
- EPS.
- Working capital.
- Depreciation.
- Capital expenditure.
- Operating cash flow.
- Free cash flow.

Use explicit assumptions and formulas.

## 3. Valuation

Build:
- Historical P/E.
- Forward P/E.
- EV/EBITDA.
- EV/Sales.
- Price-to-free-cash-flow.
- Enterprise value.
- Equity value.
- Fair value per share.

## 4. Scenario analysis

Create:
- Bear case.
- Base case.
- Bull case.

Allow assumptions to change independently.

## 5. Sensitivity analysis

Include:
- Revenue growth sensitivity.
- Margin sensitivity.
- WACC sensitivity.
- Terminal growth sensitivity.
- Valuation multiple sensitivity.

## 6. Shareholder returns

Calculate:
- Expected stock price.
- Capital appreciation.
- Dividend income.
- Total return.
- Annualized return.
- Dilution impact.

## 7. Excel requirements

Create separate worksheets:

1. Dashboard
2. Assumptions
3. Historical Financials
4. Income Statement
5. Balance Sheet
6. Cash Flow
7. Revenue Segments
8. Debt Schedule
9. Valuation
10. DCF
11. Scenario Analysis
12. Sensitivity Analysis
13. Investment Returns
14. Sources

All projections must be formula-driven, not hardcoded.

Include source references, reporting dates, units, formulas and assumption notes.

Do not fabricate missing financial information. Clearly identify estimates and management guidance.

Deliver an editable Excel workbook with an executive dashboard.
