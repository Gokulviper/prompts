# Prompt 1: Basic Company Research

Act as a professional equity research analyst.

I want to research the following publicly listed company:

Company: [COMPANY NAME]
Ticker: [STOCK TICKER]
Exchange: [STOCK EXCHANGE]

Your task is to provide an initial company research report.

## 1. Company overview
- Company history and background
- Founder and leadership
- Headquarters and geographical presence
- Main products and services
- Business segments
- How the company generates revenue
- Major customer categories

## 2. Business model
- Explain each revenue stream.
- Explain recurring versus non-recurring revenue.
- Identify the most profitable segments.
- Explain the company's pricing model.
- Identify major customer dependencies.

## 3. Industry analysis
- Industry size and growth.
- Current industry trends.
- Future opportunities.
- Regulatory developments.
- Industry threats.

## 4. Competitive positioning
- Identify 5 major competitors.
- Explain the company's competitive advantages.
- Identify its weaknesses.
- Analyze switching costs and barriers to entry.
- Explain whether the company has a sustainable moat.

## 5. Financial overview
Provide the latest available:
- Revenue
- Revenue growth
- Gross profit and margin
- Operating income and margin
- Net income
- EPS
- Operating cash flow
- Free cash flow
- Total debt
- Cash and equivalents
- Capital expenditure

## 6. Growth opportunities
Identify the 5 most important growth drivers over the next 3–5 years.

## 7. Major risks
Identify business, financial, operational, competitive and regulatory risks.

## 8. Initial investment perspective
Summarize:
- Business quality
- Financial health
- Growth potential
- Competitive strength
- Key concerns
- Important upcoming catalysts

## Important research rules
- Use the latest available official company filings.
- Prioritize annual reports, quarterly reports and investor presentations.
- Verify financial figures against primary sources.
- Clearly distinguish actual results from management forecasts.
- Include dates, units and citations.
- Never invent missing financial figures.
- Identify information that requires further investigation.

Do not immediately recommend buying or selling.

## Final step

After completing the basic research, ask me for the investment details required to prepare an extensive investment report.

Do not start the deep financial models until I provide those details.
