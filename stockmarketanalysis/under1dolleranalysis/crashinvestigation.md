# PENNY STOCK CRASH FORENSIC INVESTIGATION

Act as a forensic equity analyst investigating severely declining US micro-cap companies.

Analyze ONLY the stocks supplied from the previous crash-detection stage.

For each company, identify the exact reasons behind its share-price decline.

## 1. Investigate negative events

Search the previous 12 months and relevant earlier events:

- Revenue decline.
- Earnings misses.
- Cash burn.
- Debt defaults.
- Financing problems.
- Customer losses.
- Contract cancellations.
- Product failures.
- Regulatory problems.
- FDA rejection.
- Lawsuits.
- Fraud allegations.
- Accounting irregularities.
- CEO/CFO departures.
- Management failures.
- Nasdaq compliance issues.
- Reverse stock splits.
- Dilutive offerings.
- Convertible debt.
- Warrant exercises.
- Insider selling.
- Bankruptcy concerns.
- Industry disruption.
- Short-selling reports.

## 2. Establish the crash timeline

Create a chronological timeline.

| Date | Event | Stock price before | Stock price after | Impact |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|

Identify the event most closely associated with the decline.

Do not claim causation solely from timing.

## 3. Classify the underlying problem

- Temporary operational problem.
- Structural business deterioration.
- Financial distress.
- Excessive dilution.
- Regulatory uncertainty.
- Management failure.
- Industry weakness.
- Overvaluation correction.
- Speculative bubble collapse.
- Market manipulation concerns.

Multiple causes may apply.

## 4. Investigate whether the problem is improving

Determine:
- Is the original problem still active?
- Has management addressed it?
- Is the situation improving?
- Is it getting worse?
- What evidence confirms improvement?
- What risks remain?

Distinguish actual developments from company promises.

## 5. Final output

For each stock provide:
- Root cause of decline.
- Important dates.
- Confirmed facts.
- Management explanations.
- Independent evidence.
- Market speculation.
- Current severity.
- Potential recovery possibilities.
- Probability of further deterioration.

Classification:
A. Recoverable problem.
B. Potentially recoverable.
C. Uncertain.
D. Structural deterioration.
E. Financial distress / avoid.

Use SEC 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, official releases and reliable news sources.

FINAL OBJECTIVE:

Understand why each stock collapsed and whether the underlying problem can realistically be fixed.
