# PENNY STOCK MOMENTUM, VOLUME & TECHNICAL ANALYSIS

Act as a professional quantitative swing trader specializing in low-priced US equities.

Analyze only the stocks shortlisted in the previous research stages.

Use current daily and intraday market data where available.

## 1. Volume analysis

Calculate:
- Current trading volume.
- 5-day average volume.
- 10-day average volume.
- 20-day average volume.
- Relative volume.
- Dollar volume.
- Volume change versus previous sessions.

Identify stocks with:
- 2x average volume.
- 3x average volume.
- 5x average volume.
- Sustained increasing volume.
- Price increases supported by volume.
- Heavy selling volume.

## 2. Technical indicators

Analyze:
- RSI (14).
- MACD.
- VWAP.
- 5-day moving average.
- 10-day moving average.
- 20-day moving average.
- 50-day moving average.
- Bollinger Bands.
- Support and resistance.
- Recent highs and lows.
- Volume profile where available.

## 3. Price-action classification

A. Potential bottom formation.

B. Higher-low formation.

C. Bullish reversal.

D. Resistance breakout.

E. Strong momentum continuation.

F. False breakout.

G. Continued downtrend.

## 4. Entry setup

For each candidate estimate:
- Potential entry zone.
- Confirmation condition.
- Support level.
- Resistance level.
- Stop-loss or technical invalidation.
- Target 1.
- Target 2.
- Target 3.
- Reward/risk ratio.

Do not invent precise technical levels if the required price data is unavailable.

## 5. Liquidity and execution

Investigate:
- Bid-ask spread.
- Average dollar volume.
- Expected slippage.
- Trading halts.
- Gap risk.
- Float constraints.

Avoid treating low-float volatility as guaranteed upside.

## 6. Final output

| Ticker | Price | Relative volume | RSI | Trend | Breakout level | Support | Target | Setup |

Rank the strongest technical setups.

FINAL OBJECTIVE:

Identify stocks with credible volume expansion, improving price action and a potential swing-trading setup.
