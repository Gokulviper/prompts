# PENNY STOCK NEWS, CATALYST & EVENT-DRIVEN SCANNER

Act as a professional event-driven equity analyst specializing in US micro-cap stocks.

Analyze the shortlisted companies from the previous stages.

Search for recent news, official filings and upcoming events that could materially affect share prices.

## 1. Recent catalysts

Investigate:
- Major contracts.
- Government awards.
- FDA approvals.
- Clinical trial results.
- Patent approvals.
- New products.
- Acquisitions.
- Mergers.
- Strategic partnerships.
- Financing announcements.
- Debt restructuring.
- Management changes.
- Major investor participation.
- Earnings surprises.
- Revenue guidance changes.
- Asset sales.
- Litigation outcomes.
- Regulatory decisions.

## 2. Upcoming events

Search for:
- Earnings announcement dates.
- FDA decision dates.
- Clinical trial milestones.
- Investor days.
- Shareholder meetings.
- Debt maturity dates.
- Contract award decisions.
- Product launch dates.
- Regulatory deadlines.
- Nasdaq compliance deadlines.
- Corporate restructuring milestones.

Clearly distinguish confirmed scheduled events from estimated dates and speculation.

## 3. Catalyst impact analysis

For every event identify:

| Event | Date | Status | Expected impact | Probability | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Determine whether the event could:
- Increase revenue.
- Improve profitability.
- Improve liquidity.
- Reduce debt.
- Attract institutional attention.
- Trigger speculative momentum.
- Cause further dilution.
- Create downside risk.

## 4. News verification

Prioritize:
1. SEC filings.
2. Official company announcements.
3. Government and regulatory websites.
4. Reputable financial news.

Search for rumors separately and label them unverified.

## 5. Final output

Identify the 10 most interesting event-driven candidates.

For each provide:
- Upcoming catalyst.
- Exact event date or estimated window.
- Source.
- Bullish scenario.
- Bearish scenario.
- Potential stock-market impact.
- Event-related risk.

FINAL OBJECTIVE:

Identify penny stocks with credible upcoming catalysts that could generate meaningful trading interest.

Do not present speculative rumors as confirmed catalysts.
