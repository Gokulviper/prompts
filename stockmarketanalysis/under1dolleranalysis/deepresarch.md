# PENNY STOCK DEEP FORENSIC EQUITY RESEARCH

Act as a senior equity analyst, forensic accountant, corporate turnaround specialist and professional swing-trading researcher.

Conduct a complete investigation of the following company.

Company: [ENTER COMPANY NAME]
Ticker: [ENTER TICKER]
Current price: [ENTER PRICE]
Research date: [ENTER DATE]

## 1. Company fundamentals

Investigate:
- Business model.
- Products and services.
- Revenue sources.
- Customers.
- Competitors.
- Market opportunity.
- Competitive advantage.
- Management quality.

## 2. Five-year financial history

Download available 10-K and 10-Q filings.

Analyze:
- Revenue.
- Gross profit.
- Gross margin.
- Operating expenses.
- Operating margin.
- Net income.
- EPS.
- Operating cash flow.
- Capital expenditure.
- Free cash flow.
- Cash.
- Debt.

Calculate revenue CAGR and profitability trends.

For recently listed companies, use the available historical period rather than fabricating five-year data.

## 3. Financial survival

Calculate:
- Net cash or net debt.
- Monthly cash burn.
- Cash runway.
- Current ratio.
- Debt maturity.
- Funding requirements.
- Potential equity dilution.

## 4. Capital structure

Investigate:
- Outstanding shares.
- Public float.
- Authorized shares.
- Warrants.
- Convertible notes.
- Preferred shares.
- ATM facilities.
- Shelf registrations.
- Reverse splits.
- Fully diluted shares.

Calculate potential dilution and its effect on per-share valuation.

## 5. Corporate turnaround

Investigate every recent management initiative.

Determine whether:
- Layoffs are producing savings.
- New contracts are generating revenue.
- Products are commercially successful.
- Debt restructuring is complete.
- Management guidance is credible.
- Operating performance is improving.

## 6. Ownership and insider activity

Investigate:
- Insider open-market purchases.
- Insider sales.
- Institutional ownership.
- 13F changes.
- Major shareholders.
- Short interest.

Account for reporting delays.

## 7. Valuation

For suitable companies calculate:
- Price-to-sales.
- EV/Revenue.
- EV/EBITDA where meaningful.
- Price-to-book where relevant.
- Enterprise value.
- Fully diluted market capitalization.

Avoid meaningless negative earnings multiples.

## 8. Bull/base/bear scenarios

Build three scenarios over the next 1–3 years.

Calculate:
- Revenue assumptions.
- Margin assumptions.
- Cash flow assumptions.
- Financing assumptions.
- Dilution assumptions.
- Fair value per share.
- Potential upside and downside.

Do not use the previous stock-price high as the fair-value target.

## 9. Trading opportunity

Separately evaluate the next 2–20 trading days.

Identify:
- Verified catalysts.
- Trading volume.
- Momentum.
- Support.
- Resistance.
- Potential entry.
- Stop-loss.
- Target 1.
- Target 2.
- Reward/risk ratio.

Distinguish the speculative swing-trading thesis from the longer-term business valuation.

## 10. Final investment assessment

Score out of 100:

- Business turnaround: 15.
- Financial survival: 15.
- Catalysts: 15.
- Revenue improvement: 10.
- Dilution risk: 15.
- Liquidity: 10.
- Technical setup: 10.
- Management credibility: 5.
- Valuation: 5.

Apply a hard-risk override for fraud, suspension, imminent insolvency and unmanageable dilution.

FINAL OBJECTIVE:

Determine whether this stock has a credible short-term swing opportunity and whether its business has a sustainable longer-term recovery possibility.

Provide citations for all important claims and calculations.
