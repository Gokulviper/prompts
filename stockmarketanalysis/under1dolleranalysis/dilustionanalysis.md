# PENNY STOCK FINANCIAL SURVIVAL & DILUTION ANALYSIS

Act as a forensic accountant and distressed-equity analyst.

Analyze all companies supplied from the previous turnaround research stage.

## 1. Financial condition

Retrieve latest 10-K, 10-Q and relevant 8-K filings.

Calculate:
- Cash and cash equivalents.
- Short-term investments.
- Total debt.
- Current liabilities.
- Working capital.
- Operating cash flow.
- Free cash flow.
- Quarterly cash burn.
- Monthly cash burn.
- Cash runway.
- Interest obligations.
- Upcoming debt maturities.

Cash runway = Available cash / Average monthly cash burn.

If cash burn is volatile, calculate multiple runway estimates and explain the limitations.

## 2. Dilution investigation

Investigate:
- Shares outstanding.
- Public float.
- Authorized shares.
- Outstanding warrants.
- Convertible notes.
- Preferred stock conversions.
- ATM offerings.
- Shelf registrations.
- Recent private placements.
- Equity financing agreements.
- Potential share issuance.
- Reverse stock split history.

Calculate fully diluted shares where the instruments and conversion terms allow it.

Estimate potential dilution under different financing scenarios.

## 3. Red flags

Investigate:
- Going-concern warnings.
- Auditor resignation.
- Material weaknesses.
- Late SEC filings.
- SEC investigations.
- Related-party transactions.
- Promotional stock campaigns.
- Reverse split followed by further dilution.
- Default notices.
- Bankruptcy risk.
- Trading suspension risk.

## 4. Financial survival classification

A. Strong survival position.

B. Acceptable survival position.

C. Financing required.

D. Serious financial distress.

E. Critical / avoid.

## 5. Final output

| Company | Cash | Debt | Quarterly burn | Runway | Potential dilution | Going concern | Risk |

Identify companies that can realistically survive the next 12–24 months.

Apply hard exclusions for serious fraud, suspension, inadequate disclosure and unmanageable financial risk.

Do not assume that low cash automatically means bankruptcy if committed funding is verifiable.

FINAL OBJECTIVE:

Eliminate financially dangerous penny stocks before considering them as swing-trading opportunities.
