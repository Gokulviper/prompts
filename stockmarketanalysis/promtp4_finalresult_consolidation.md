# Prompt 4: Final Institutional Investment Committee Report

Act as the chair of an institutional investment committee.

You have completed the following research:

1. Complete Excel valuation model.
2. Five-year financial statement analysis.
3. Detailed DCF valuation.
4. Competitor valuation comparison.

Company: [COMPANY NAME]
Ticker: [TICKER]

Investment horizon: [HORIZON]
Investment capital: [AMOUNT]
Risk tolerance: [RISK PROFILE]

Your task is to challenge and reconcile all four research projects.

## 1. Validate the research

- Check financial data consistency.
- Identify conflicting assumptions.
- Verify share counts.
- Check debt and cash adjustments.
- Identify double-counted liabilities.
- Reconcile GAAP and non-GAAP earnings.
- Check forecast calculations.

## 2. Investment thesis

Explain:
- Why this company could create shareholder value.
- What could destroy shareholder value.
- Main growth drivers.
- Main financial risks.
- Competitive position.

## 3. Valuation reconciliation

Compare:
- DCF valuation.
- P/E valuation.
- EV/EBITDA valuation.
- Peer valuation.
- Historical valuation.

Explain why valuations differ.

## 4. Scenario analysis

Present:
- Bear case.
- Base case.
- Bull case.

Include:
- Share price.
- Fair value.
- Upside/downside.
- Key assumptions.
- Probability assumptions, clearly labelled as analyst estimates if used.

## 5. Investor returns

Calculate returns for the stated investment amount.

Include:
- Initial capital.
- Number of shares.
- Brokerage and currency conversion costs.
- Dividend income.
- INR/USD movement.
- Tax assumptions.
- Total expected INR return.

## 6. Investment monitoring

Define:
- Quarterly KPIs.
- Positive business signals.
- Negative business signals.
- Thesis invalidation conditions.
- Important upcoming events.

## 7. Final investment committee conclusion

Present:
- Strongest reasons to invest.
- Strongest reasons not to invest.
- Valuation uncertainty.
- Key risks.
- Conditions that would justify a position.
- Conditions that would invalidate the thesis.

Do not simply average valuation estimates.

Challenge assumptions and identify the most reliable valuation method for this company.

Do not manufacture certainty or guarantee returns.
